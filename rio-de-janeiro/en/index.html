Our experiences

Centro Histórico e Lapa

After admiring the magnificent exterior facade of the theater and learning about the history of Cinelândia, we will continue our free walking tour of Rio de Janeiro along Avenida Rio Branco toward Confeitaria Colombo.

There, you will discover why this confectionery is such a popular tourist attraction. We will also pass by other iconic sites, such as the Arcos do Telles, the elegant Paço Imperial, and the beautiful Old Cathedral of Rio de Janeiro, and finally Lapa and its diversity.

2h30 · Minimum 3 people · Segunda, Quarta e Quinta e Sábado · 10:30 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

Santa Teresa & Experiência Gastronômica Carioca

The bohemian heart of Rio de Janeiro awaits you. We will begin our journey by boarding the traditional yellow Santa Teresa streetcars, an icon of the Brazilian Belle Époque. As the streetcar winds through the charming cobblestone streets, you will discover beautiful mansions and several vibrant street-art murals.

Santa Teresa is known as the birthplace of Rio's bohemian scene, where creativity and joy meet on every corner. Along the way, we will enjoy privileged views of the famous Christ the Redeemer statue, and you will be swept up by the vibrant atmosphere of the neighborhood, filled with artists' studios, charming secondhand bookstores, and traditional bars that tell stories on their walls.

Amid colonial houses and grand mansions, we will arrive at Parque das Ruínas. There, we can enter the house of Laurinda Santos Lobo — a mansion that remained abandoned for more than 50 years, but once embodied the splendor of Rio. This revitalized space offers a spectacular panoramic view of the city and provides a glimpse into life during the Brazilian Empire.

To complete your immersion in Rio's culture, there is nothing better than an authentic gastronomic experience. Enjoy unique and traditional flavors at one of the many restaurants and bars that make Santa Teresa a true paradise for lovers of good food. Savor the local cuisine and feel the essence of the bohemian scene in every bite, in a setting that blends history, art, and flavor in an unforgettable way.

Todos os dias · 10:00,14:00 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

Pedra do Sal: Samba e Herança Afro-brasileira

Geography says that the Atlantic Ocean separates Africa from Brazil. However, Rio's music, architecture, and art suggest otherwise. We will meet in front of the Museum of Art of Rio at 4:00 pm to begin our incredible free tour of the city's African cultural heritage.

First, we will visit Praça Mauá, where you will find the elegant avant-garde architecture of the Museum of Tomorrow.

This state-of-the-art building, designed by Spanish architect Santiago Calatrava, points toward the city's future while preserving its African roots—an essential aspect for understanding Rio's history.

3h · Minimum 3 people · Segunda e Sexta · 16:00 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

Santa Teresa Arte e Boêmia

The bohemian heart of Rio de Janeiro awaits you. During this free walking tour through the Santa Teresa neighborhood, you will discover beautiful mansions, charming yellow streetcars, and countless urban art murals. We will begin by walking along the cobblestone streets while talking about the history of the area. Did you know that its bohemian reputation is due to the wave of artists who moved there in the 1960s? During the tour, we will enjoy privileged views of Rio de Janeiro's iconic Christ the Redeemer. Passing colonial houses, palaces, and Santa Teresa's famous yellow streetcars, we will arrive at Parque das Ruínas. There, we can enter the house of Laurinda Santos Lobo, a mansion abandoned for more than 50 years that once represented the splendor of the Brazilian Belle Époque. The restored space offers a spectacular panoramic view of Rio de Janeiro and shows us what life was like during the former Brazilian Empire. This guided visit will include a tour of the main urban-art murals. Remember, we are in a place full of artists.

2h30 · 3 people · Quarta e Domingo · 11:30,15:00 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

Pedra do Sal + Experiência de Carnaval

Explore the most famous historic sites of Afro-Rio de Janeiro, immersing yourself in Afro-Brazilian culture, gastronomy, and traditions. On this walking tour, you will learn about important Black figures who fought with all their strength to end slavery in Brazil, at emblematic places of resistance and culture. You will discover the beautiful history of resistance behind samba and, of course, finish by visiting the behind-the-scenes of a Rio de Janeiro Samba School. Get a closer look at the greatest open-air celebration in the world.

4h · Todos os dias · 10:00,14:00 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

Tour Bossa Nova: Copacabana e Ipanema

At the agreed time, we will meet you at General Osório metro station to begin this free tour of Copacabana and Ipanema. You will discover two of Rio's most famous neighborhoods!

We will begin by walking along the promenade of Ipanema Beach. We will admire the imposing mountains surrounding this bay of white sands and see the Cagarras Islands. As you appreciate the beauty of this Rio setting, you will understand why it was declared a World Heritage Site.

Next, we will visit Arpoador Beach, a meeting point for surfers. Did you know this is one of the best places to watch the sunset in Rio? Nearby, we will find Garota de Ipanema Park and Copacabana Fort, the last major fortress built in the city.

2h30 · Minimum 3 people · Quinta · 15:30 · Português, Inglês, Espanhol

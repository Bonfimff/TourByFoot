Our experiences

Passeio de Dia Inteiro - Duas Lagoas (Azul e Bonita)

Meet the guide at your hotel in the morning. Then relax on a jeep ride from Barreirinhas to Lençóis Maranhenses National Park, known for its vast sand dunes and desert-like landscapes. Upon reaching the park, start the walk from one lagoon and cross the sand to another lagoon. Stop at a local restaurant in the middle of an oasis to enjoy a delicious lunch. After the meal, head out to admire the beautiful sunset over the dunes and watch the sky change color as the sun sets. Finally, return to the jeep and head back to your hotel in Barreirinhas to end the activity.

Up to 9 people · Todos os dias · 08:30 · Português

Tour Santo Amaro (Circuito Betânia)

The itinerary includes the beautiful Betânia Lagoon, located in the small village of the same name. The lagoon has an extensive formation and is bordered by a beautiful river, with the two bodies of water separated by a large dune that offers an excellent viewpoint over the landscape.

The tour starts at 09:00, with three stops at lagoons for leisure and swimming. Beach umbrellas, chairs, and ice are provided for the group.

Afterward, there is a lunch stop. To reach the restaurant, a boat crossing fee must be paid, which is not included in the package. Alternatively, you can return to town for lunch.

Up to 9 people · Todos os dias · 09:00 · Português

Manhã na Lagoa Azul

This is a half-day excursion to the Lençóis Maranhenses.

We start picking up passengers at hotels in Barreirinhas at around 08:30. Once everyone is on board, we travel for about one hour to the Lençóis Maranhenses.

We will walk across the park's dunes, and from the top you will have a panoramic view of its stunning scenery.

Then it is time to explore the lagoons. Your guide will take you on a short walk through the area to visit two lagoons, where you will have free time to swim, about 30 minutes at each lagoon.

On this tour, you will visit either the Lagoa Azul Circuit or the Lagoa da Prata Circuit, depending on the water depth conditions at different times of the year.

The pick-up time is approximate. Please contact us so we can adjust the pick-up details.

Todos os dias · 08:30 · Português

Transfer de São Luís à Barreirinhas

Departure from Barreirinhas to São Luís by air-conditioned minibus or van. The transfer is operated by a professional driver and takes approximately 4 hours and 30 minutes, with one stop midway through the journey to eat and use the restroom.

The service includes round-trip transportation to inns, hotels, and hostels located within the urban area. Along the way, you may enjoy a beautiful sunset over the desert and the rural landscapes on the return to Barreirinhas.

The selected boarding time is the departure time of the trip, but pick-up may begin up to one hour earlier. Be ready to leave one hour before the selected time.

Each passenger is entitled to one piece of luggage weighing up to 10 kg. An extra fee will be charged for additional luggage.

Tour Lagoa Bonita (Entardecer)

This is a half-day excursion to Lençóis with a sunset experience.

We start picking up passengers at hotels in Barreirinhas at around 14:00. Once everyone is on board, we travel for about one hour to the Lagoa Bonita tourist center.

There, we will find a small village with local businesses, such as handicraft souvenir shops and family-run snack bars, as well as access to the famous Lagoa Bonita staircase.

At the top of the staircase, you will have a panoramic view of the park's stunning scenery.

Then it is time to explore the lagoons. Your guide will take you on a short guided walk through the area to visit two or three lagoons, where you will have free time to swim, about 40 minutes at each lagoon.

Finally, you will climb the highest dune in the region to enjoy the incredible sunset over Lençóis before returning to the city.

The pick-up time is approximate. Please contact us so we can adjust the pick-up details.

Santo Amaro: Circuito Rancharia

Departure is at 09:00 toward the hostel. The journey is made in 4x4 vehicles and takes a maximum of 35 minutes per dune. Upon arriving in Rancharia, the vehicle stops near the mountains, and a short walk is required. To reach the main destination, you can choose a boat ride, which takes about 10 minutes, or a walk through a flooded area to the lagoon, which takes about 15 minutes. Finally, you will be rewarded with a spectacular view of Lagoa Bicudo Gordo. The vastness of the lagoon and the sand dunes that form the famous lobes look like a movie set. In addition to the privilege of seeing the Lençóis Maranhenses from a 360° perspective, the water is so crystal clear that you will not want to leave. The tour lasts all day, with a lunch break in a nearby village. Finally, there will be a stop at other lagoons for a refreshing final swim, followed by a return to the highest point to enjoy the sunset.

Quadriciclo Adventure (Combo lancha + quadri)

The ATV tour to Caburé is a full-day trip. You will cover about 80 km round trip, passing through restinga vegetation, cerrado, dunes and lagoons of the Pequenos Lençóis, as well as sections of beach. The tour is carried out in a group, with the tour guide in front and the participants following behind. The final destination is Caburé Beach. The tour ends at the end of the day, on the way back, with a stop in the Vassouras community. The price is per ATV, with capacity for up to 2 people.

Passeio de lancha no rio Preguicas - Caburé

In the morning, we will pick you up at your hotel in Barreirinhas before heading to the picturesque Preguiças River. The adventure toward paradise begins on paved roads in a 4x4 vehicle until we reach the port of Barreirinhas. Once aboard our speedboat, you will begin discovering the different landscapes along the banks of the region's most imposing river. We will make stops so you can walk through the dunes of Pequenos Lençóis, visit the Vassouras community, and see the Mandacaru Lighthouse. Finally, lunch will be in Caburé, a place that connects the Atlantic Ocean with our majestic river.

Free time and sunset at the lagoons

End of the day

(Return after sunset)

3 - Day Trekking Tour to Santo Amaro (Super trekking)

SUPER TREKKING

FIRST DAY - APPROXIMATELY 14 km

Departure from Barreirinhas:

3:30 am: Board a 4x4 vehicle heading to the Lagoa Bonita circuit. A hearty breakfast will be served before the tour begins.

Start of the hike:

At sunrise, with the golden morning light illuminating the dunes, we will begin a journey of approximately 14 km through the breathtaking landscapes of Lençóis Maranhenses.

During the tour, we will make strategic stops for swimming in crystal-clear lagoons, making the most of the natural scenery.

Arrival at the Mucambo oasis

Our first stop will be the charming Mucambo oasis, where we will be welcomed by local families. There, we will enjoy a traditional homemade lunch prepared with typical regional ingredients.

Free afternoon and sunset at the lagoons

After lunch, you will have free time to rest and relax. At 4:30 pm, we will take a short walk to explore nearby lagoons and admire a magnificent sunset over the dunes.

End of the day

Dinner will be served at 7:30 pm. You will have free time to light a campfire, talk, admire the starry sky, or simply rest and recharge for the next day's adventure.

SECOND DAY - APPROXIMATELY 16 km

Start of the day: 5:00 am.

We begin the day very early with a simple and nutritious breakfast, followed by the start of the trail at sunrise, when the landscape takes on golden tones and the temperature is more pleasant for hiking.

Morning hike and swimming in the lagoons (6 km)

We will walk approximately 6 km through the dunes, with several stops for swimming in crystal-clear lagoons. The highlight of the morning will be arriving at Barraca do Diel, a rustic structure built on the dunes beside a beautiful lagoon.

Swimming and lunch break

At Barraca do Diel we will stop for swimming and rest, then have lunch and at 2:00 pm depart for the next destination.

Afternoon hike to the Rancharia oasis (10 km)

After lunch and a short rest, we will resume the hike at 2:00 pm, continuing another 10 km toward our second support point: the Rancharia oasis.

End of the day

At Rancharia, you will be welcomed by local families, where we will have dinner and spend the night. The setting is simple, welcoming, and surrounded by nature, ideal for recharging and preparing for the next day of adventure.

THIRD DAY - APPROXIMATELY 14 km

Start of the day: 4:00 am.

We will wake up early for a light breakfast and, at 4:40 am, begin the trail while it is still light, taking advantage of the mild temperature and the beauty of sunrise over the dunes.

Trail to the Betânia region (17 km)

This will be the longest day of the crossing: a hike of about 17 km to Lagoa do Junco in the Betânia region. We will stop for a swim and then continue to Santo Amaro by 4x4 vehicle for lunch.

Lunch in Betânia

Upon arrival, we will stop for a well-deserved regional lunch, with time to relax and enjoy the last views of Lençóis Maranhenses.

Return to Barreirinhas

At 2:00 pm, we will board a vehicle that will take us to Barreirinhas.

The journey between Santo Amaro and Barreirinhas takes an average of 1 hour and 30 minutes.

Note:

The estimated return time to Barreirinhas is 2:00 pm.

If you need a transfer to São Luís, the transfer departure time is 2:00 pm (Request the service in advance).
